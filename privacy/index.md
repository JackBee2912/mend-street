# Mend Street: Repair Stall — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 November 2026
**Contact:** jackbee.tech@gmail.com

## What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Mend Street: Repair Stall** ("the game") handles, why, and what choices you
have. The game has no accounts and no server of its own.

## Information the game handles

**Game progress.** Your progress (story, coins, items, settings) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. If you
delete the game, it is erased and cannot be recovered.

**Advertising.** The game is free and paid for by advertising. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which works with
Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these services may collect your device's
advertising identifier, device information (model, operating system, language), your approximate location from your IP
address, and how you interact with ads.

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states that require it, the game asks for your
  consent before any ad is personalised (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in the
  game's settings. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
- On iPhone and iPad, the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency whether apps may track you. If you say no,
  ads are not personalised and your advertising identifier is not used.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device's settings, change your ad consent in the
game's settings, and erase everything the game keeps by deleting it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date.
