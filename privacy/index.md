# Mend Street: Repair Stall — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026
**Contact:** jackbee.tech@gmail.com

## What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Mend Street: Repair Stall** ("the game") handles, why, and what
choices you have. The game has no accounts and no server of its own.

## Information the game handles

**Game progress.** Your progress (story, coins, items, settings) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. If you
delete the game, it is erased and cannot be recovered.

**Advertising.** The game is free and paid for by advertising, served by Unity Ads: short videos you can choose to watch
for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break (handing a repair back, going to sleep). The game shows
**non-personalised ads only**: it tells Unity Ads that you have not consented to personalised advertising, and it never
asks to track you. To show ads, count them, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect your
device's advertising identifier, device information (model, operating system, language), your approximate location from
your IP address, and how you interact with the ads.

**Analytics.** The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, so it can be made better:
for example how far players get in the season, which activities they try and where they stop. It records game events
(such as a repair finished or a day started), an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, device information (model,
operating system, language, app version) and your approximate location from your IP address. It does not record your
name, contacts or anything you type. The game turns off advertising uses of this data: ad storage, ad user data and ad
personalisation are denied, so analytics data is never used to target ads at you.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's settings, and erase
everything the game keeps on your device by deleting it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date.
