# Mend Street: 365 Days — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026
**Contact:** jackbee.tech@gmail.com

## What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Mend Street: 365 Days** ("the game") handles, why, and what
choices you have. The game has no accounts and no server of its own.

## Information the game handles

**Game progress.** Your progress (story, coins, items, settings) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. If you
delete the game, it is erased and cannot be recovered.

**Advertising.** The game is free and paid for mainly by advertising, served by Unity Ads: short videos you can choose to watch
for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break (handing a repair back, going to sleep). The game shows
**non-personalised ads only**: it tells Unity Ads that you have not consented to personalised advertising, and it never
asks to track you. To show ads, count them, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect your
device's advertising identifier, device information (model, operating system, language), your approximate location from
your IP address, and how you interact with the ads.

**Analytics (you can turn it off).** On Android the game asks the first time you open it whether it may send anonymous
play statistics, and nothing is sent unless you choose Agree. On iPhone the statistics are on from the start. On both you
can turn them off at any time in Settings > Play statistics, and nothing more is sent after that. When they are on, the
game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, so it can be made better:
for example how far players get in the season, which activities they try and where they stop. It records game events
(such as a repair finished or a day started), an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, device information (model,
operating system, language, app version) and your approximate location from your IP address. It does not record your
name, contacts or anything you type. The game turns off advertising uses of this data: ad storage, ad user data and ad
personalisation are denied, so analytics data is never used to target ads at you.

**Purchases (No Ads).** From version 1.0.1 the game sells one optional item, No Ads, which turns off the full-screen ads at natural breaks
(the ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available). The purchase is made and paid for entirely through Google Play
Billing on Android or the App Store on iPhone; the game never sees your payment details. It receives only whether the
purchase went through, so it can turn the ads off and give the purchase back if you reinstall (Settings > Restore
purchases). When play statistics are on, Google Analytics for Firebase also records that a purchase was made (the item and
its price), as part of the statistics described above.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can turn play statistics on or off in the game's Settings, report an inappropriate ad from Settings > Report an ad,
reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's settings, and erase everything the
game keeps on your device by deleting it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date.
